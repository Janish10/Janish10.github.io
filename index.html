Hacker by mindset.

Researcher by practice.

I'm a B.Tech student in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science at St. Joseph's Institute of Technology (Anna University), Chennai. My focus sits at the intersection of offensive security, vulnerability research, and building tools that solve real problems.

I hunt bugs across web applications through bug bounty programs, reverse engineer binaries, and compete in CTF challenges as a core member of Team Ex0rcists. I've been acknowledged by Zoho, Nokia Security, and CERT-In for responsible disclosure, and hold three assigned CVEs for zero-day vulnerabilities in Quantum Networks.

When I'm not breaking things, I'm building them — from AI-powered fraud detection systems to production room-booking platforms. I bring an attacker's perspective to everything I build.
